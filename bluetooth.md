Privacy Policy for Bluetooth Scanner
Last Updated: 09/18/2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Bluetooth Scanner" app (referred to as "the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles your information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. The core principle of our policy is simple:

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or user information.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect
The Bluetooth Scanner app is designed to function without needing any information from you. Specifically, we do not:

Collect Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

Access Device Identifiers: We do not access, collect, or transmit your device's unique identifiers.

Track Location: We do not access, collect, or track your location data.

Log Usage Data: We do not collect analytics on how you use the App (often called "usage data").

Scan or Access Personal Data: The App's functionality is limited to scanning for Bluetooth device names, addresses, and signal strength. It does not access the content on your device, such as photos, contacts, files, or messages.

2. Information the App Accesses (And Why)
To perform its core function of scanning for nearby Bluetooth devices, the App requires certain permissions on your device. The data accessed is used only locally on your device and is not collected by us.

Bluetooth Permission: This permission is required to discover and list nearby Bluetooth devices (e.g., headphones, speakers, smartwatches). The list of devices, their names, and signal strengths are displayed only to you within the App and are not recorded or sent to us.

3. Data Sharing
Since we do not collect any data, we have no data to share with any third parties, partners, or affiliates.

4. Data Security
We implement industry-standard practices to protect the App itself. As we do not collect or store any user data, there is no risk of your information being compromised by a breach of our servers.

5. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. We are compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.

7. Your Consent
