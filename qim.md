Privacy Policy for Quick Invoice Maker
Last Updated: October 22, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "Quick Invoice Maker" (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). Your privacy is critically important to us, and this policy is designed to inform you about our practices concerning your personal data.

2. Data Collection and Storage

The core principle of our App's operation is user privacy. Therefore:

We Do Not Collect Any Data. The Quick Invoice Maker app does not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal information or user data on external servers.

Local-Only Storage. All data you create within the App—including but not limited to invoice details, client information, item lists, and prices—is stored exclusively on your personal device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.

3. Data Sharing

Since we do not collect any data, we do not and cannot share any of your data with any third parties, advertisers, or analytics companies.

4. User-Initiated Sharing

The App includes functionality that allows you to share your created invoices (e.g., via email, messaging apps, or cloud services). This action is initiated solely by you. When you share an invoice, you are responsible for the data you send and the privacy practices of the platform or service you use to share it. We have no control over and are not responsible for the data handling practices of third-party services.

5. Data Security

As all your data resides locally on your device, its security is dependent on the security measures of your device itself (e.g., device passcode, biometric locks, and operating system security). We recommend you maintain the security of your device to prevent unauthorized access to the App and its data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
