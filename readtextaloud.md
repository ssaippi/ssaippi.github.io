Privacy Policy for "Read Text Aloud"
Last Updated: 06/27/2025

1. Introduction
"Read Text Aloud" (the "App") is developed to convert text to speech locally on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our App.

2. Data Collection
The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

Personal Information (name, email, contacts, etc.)

Text Input (all processing happens locally on your device)

Voice Recordings (speech synthesis is performed in real-time without saving)

Location Data

Device Identifiers

3. Permissions
The App may request the following permissions:

Microphone Access (only if you enable live speech features in future updates)

Speech Synthesis (required for text-to-speech functionality)
No permissions are used to collect personal data.

4. Third-Party Services
The App uses:

Apple's AVFoundation Framework (for text-to-speech conversion)

No analytics or advertising SDKs

All processing occurs on-device, and no data is sent to external servers.

5. Children’s Privacy
The App is safe for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted here.
