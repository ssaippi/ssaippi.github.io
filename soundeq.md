Thank you for using Sound Equalizer (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our App.

1. Data Collection and Usage
The Sound Equalizer App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from your device.

Audio Processing: The App processes audio files locally on your device to apply equalizer effects. No audio data is sent to external servers or third parties.

File Access: The App allows you to select audio files from your device for playback and equalization. These files are accessed only for immediate playback and are not stored or uploaded.

No User Tracking: We do not track your activity, usage patterns, or personal information.

2. Permissions
The App may request the following permissions:

Media Access: To allow you to select and play audio files stored on your device.

Audio Playback Control: To enable background playback and control via lock screen/Control Center.

These permissions are used solely for the App's functionality and are not used to collect data.

3. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
