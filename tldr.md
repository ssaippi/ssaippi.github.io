Privacy Policy for TLDR • Yap Assistant
Last Updated: 10/31/2025

1. Introduction
TLDR • Yap Translator ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Subscription Information: Purchase history and subscription status through the App Store

User Content
Text Input: Text you manually enter for summarization

Images: Photos you select from your library for text extraction

Screenshot Content: Images containing text that you process through the app

Technical Information
Device Information: Device type, operating system version

Usage Data: App features used, frequency of use, and performance data

3. How We Use Your Information
Text and Image Processing
Local Processing: Text extraction from images is performed locally on your device using Apple's Vision framework

AI Summarization: Text content is sent to DeepSeek API for summarization and response generation

Temporary Storage: Text is temporarily processed and not permanently stored

Subscription Features
The following features require a subscription:

Summarizing messages from screenshots

Unlimited text summaries

Unlimited AI-generated responses

Service Providers
We use third-party services including:

DeepSeek API: For AI-powered text analysis and summarization

Apple App Store: For subscription management and in-app purchases

4. Data Storage and Retention
Text Data: Processed text is not stored on our servers after processing

Images: Selected images are processed locally and not uploaded to our servers

Subscription Data: Managed through Apple's secure subscription services

Temporary Cache: Some data may be temporarily cached for app performance
