Welcome to Tracking Device Detector ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application. The App is designed to help detect Bluetooth tracking devices in your vicinity.

2. Information We Do Not Collect
We want to be completely transparent about our data practices:

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you or your devices

We do not track your location or store location data

We do not access your contacts, photos, or other personal data on your device

We do not sell or share any data with third parties because we don't collect any

3. Bluetooth Scanning
The App uses Bluetooth technology to:

Scan for nearby Bluetooth devices

Analyze signal strength (RSSI) to estimate proximity

Identify potential tracking devices based on common characteristics

All scanning and processing occurs locally on your device. No Bluetooth device information is transmitted from your device.

4. Permissions
The App requires the following permissions:

Bluetooth: To scan for nearby Bluetooth devices

Location (iOS): Required by iOS for Bluetooth scanning (we do not access or use your location data)

5. Audio
The App may play alert sounds when suspicious devices are detected. These sounds are generated locally on your device and we do not record or process any audio from your device's microphone.

6. Data Security
Since we don't collect or store your data, there is no risk of data breach or unauthorized access to your information through our App.
